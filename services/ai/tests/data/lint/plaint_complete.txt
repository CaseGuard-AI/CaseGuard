SUIT FOR DECLARATION, POSSESSION AND PERMANENT INJUNCTION

The plaintiff above-named respectfully submits as under:

1. That the plaintiff is the lawful owner of House No. B-22, Block 13, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Karachi, measuring 240 square yards, bounded on the north by House No. B-21, on the south by a 30-feet road, on the east by House No. B-23 and on the west by a lane (the suit property).

2. That in January 2026 the defendant, without any lawful authority, took possession of the ground floor of the suit property while the plaintiff was abroad.

3. That the plaintiff served a legal notice on the defendant on 10.02.2026, but the defendant refused to vacate the suit property.

4. That the cause of action first arose on 15.01.2026 when the defendant unlawfully occupied the suit property, and again on 10.02.2026 when he refused to vacate it, and it is continuing.

5. That the suit property is situated within the territorial jurisdiction of this Court and the defendant resides in Karachi, so this Court has jurisdiction to try this suit.

6. That the suit is valued at Rs. 5,000,000 for the purposes of jurisdiction, and court fee of Rs. 15,000 has been paid on the plaint as required by law.

PRAYER

It is, therefore, respectfully prayed that this Court may be pleased to pass a decree in favour of the plaintiff and against the defendant:

a) declaring that the plaintiff is the lawful owner of the suit property;

b) directing the defendant to hand over vacant possession of the suit property to the plaintiff;

c) permanently restraining the defendant from creating any third-party interest in the suit property;

d) granting costs of the suit and any other relief this Court deems fit.
